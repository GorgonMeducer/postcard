Once upon a time there was a person named Test with a cup. Test liked to drink his coffee and it made him feel happy. But one day Test was feeling very tired and he wanted to take a nap.
Test went to his bedroom and lay down. He closed his eyes and soon he was asleep. While he was sleeping, a big noise woke him up. Test opened his eyes and he saw a big truck coming towards him! Test was so scared, he screamed!
The truck driver stopped and asked Test, "Are you okay?" Test shook his head and said, "No, I'm not okay. I was very tired."
The driver was very sorry. He said, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to wake you up."
Test was still very sad, but he was still very sleepy. So he lay down on his bed and fell asleep, still feeling very tired. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big tree with a swing. Lily wanted to play on the swing. She sat on the swing and started to move back and forth. She was very happy.
